Wash Up Owner – information for App Review (Guideline 2.1)

1. SCREEN RECORDING
The attached recording was captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at app launch and shows: sign-in with the demo account, the home dashboard, the live board, orders (open an order, change its status), reports, More → reviews (reply to and report a review), staff, language switch and sign-out.
There is no account registration in the app (see point 3), so no sign-up flow is shown.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Wash Up is a car wash marketplace: customers book washes in the Wash Up customer app, and independent car washes join Wash Up as partners. Wash Up Owner is the partner app for those car wash owners and their branch managers. It lets them follow each branch in real time – incoming bookings, cars inside the branch, order status from arrival to hand-over, revenue and reports – and manage their services, prices, offers, schedule and staff from their phone instead of the web dashboard.
The app is meant for any car wash that partners with Wash Up, not for one company or its employees.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
- Sign in with the demo account in the Sign-In Information section. The country picker on the login screen is set to Egypt (+20) by default; keep it and enter the phone number exactly as given, then the password.
- Accounts are created during Wash Up partner onboarding, and an owner can add staff for their own wash from More → Staff. There is no public sign-up in the app. An owner can deactivate a staff account at any time from More → Staff.
- Main features after sign-in: Home (revenue and key numbers per branch), Live (cars in the branch), Orders (bookings; open one to confirm, start, complete or cancel it), Reports, and More (schedule, services, offers, staff, shifts, customers, reviews, finance, branches, audit log, settings, language, dark mode, sign out).
- The interface is Arabic by default; English is in More → Language.
- Customer reviews are shown in More → Reviews. The owner can reply to a review or report it to Wash Up for moderation.
- Payments are collected in person at the wash (cash or card); the app only records them. There are no in-app purchases and no digital goods.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Wash Up's own backend API (hosted on Vercel) for sign-in, data and all business actions.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (with Apple Push Notification service) for order notifications.
- The iPhone's phone app, to call a customer from an order (tel: link).
No third-party sign-in, no payment processor, no analytics or AI services.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. Wash Up currently operates with car washes in Arab countries, so the login screen accepts phone numbers from Arab League countries; features and content do not change by region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Car wash management is not a regulated industry, and the app contains no protected third-party content.
